Getting started

  1. Enter your Twitch channel In the card below. The game reads its chat; nobody in chat has to do anything.
  2. Add the overlay to OBS Sources, +, Browser. Paste this address and set the size to 1920 × 1080:
  3. Turn on the background layer From then on chatters earn ticks, join guilds and develop the land.
  4. Run a test battle Check that it shows in OBS. It changes nothing in the game.

All set. Start a War, a Guild Clash or a Pachinko round when chat is lively. The Status tab helps when something does not show up.

    War

    Three battles, one per guild as attacker. A winning attacker takes the tile, which keeps half its development.

    Hero Clash

    Guild Clash

    A countdown: everyone who writes in chat can be drawn, 10 per guild. The line-up is shown, then 3 per guild fight at a time and the others step in. The last guild standing wins the Hero Clash reward. Levels and land do not count.

    God events

    Skills

    The god casts one of the enabled skills every few seconds. Its health adjusts to the skills, so the fight stays even.

    Picture

    Fights for fun. Nothing here changes the game: no tiles, no Monument, no XP.

    Free-for-all

    A countdown: everyone who writes in chat can be drawn, 30 in all. The line-up is shown, then 10 fight at a time, everyone for themselves, and the others step in. The last one standing wins.

    Pachinko

    A countdown, drawn like a Guild Clash: up to 10 chatters per guild. Each guild gets a board, every chatter drops down it once into a slot worth points, and the guild with the most points wins. A guild with fewer chatters drops some of them twice, so every guild drops as many balls.

    Stream Deck and hotkeys

    Each link does one thing, like pressing the button here. On a Stream Deck, add a Website action, paste the link and tick GET request in background. In OBS, the script tools/obs/chroma-clash-hotkeys.py turns the links into hotkeys (Tools, Scripts).

    The links contain a secret key: anyone who has them can start fights. Do not show them on stream.

    Obstacles

    Walls, pinball bumpers that fling fighters away, rolling barrels and spike traps. About two in five team battles get a layout of their own; in a free-for-all and the endless mode they appear and disappear.

    Map banner in battles

    During every battle a strip across the screen shows the map and each guild's land and Monument. The arena keeps clear of it: no fighter, skill or skill name ever covers it.
